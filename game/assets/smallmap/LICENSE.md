# Small-map kit

Deadnight's own models, made for this game by `tools/blender/build_smallmap_walls.py`, `build_kit_stairs.py` (and the
other small-map kit build scripts) from Blender primitives: no third-party model, texture or reference geometry is in
them. The reference images the kit was sized against stay outside the repository and nothing in them was traced or
copied. They are under the game's licence (the repository's `LICENSE`).
