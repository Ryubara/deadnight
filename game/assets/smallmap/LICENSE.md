# Small-map kit

Deadnight's own models and textures, made for this game by `tools/blender/build_smallmap_walls.py`,
`tools/blender/build_kit_stairs.py`, `tools/blender/build_smallmap_lights.py` (and the other small-map kit build
scripts) from Blender primitives, and by `tools/world/build_chalk_messages.py` from drawn strokes: no third-party model,
texture, font or reference geometry is in them. The reference images the kit was sized against stay outside the
repository and nothing in them was traced or copied. They are under the game's licence (the repository's `LICENSE`).
