# Small-map kit

Deadnight's own models and textures for the second map, made for this game by the scripts in `tools/blender/` (the
kit's build scripts on `smallmap_kit.py`) and `tools/world/build_chalk_messages.py` from Blender primitives and drawn
strokes: no third-party model, texture, font or reference geometry is in them. They are under the game's licence (the
repository's `LICENSE`).
