# Modular Soldier parts

- Base: "Character Soldier" by Quaternius, from the Toon Shooter Game Kit
- Source: <https://poly.pizza/m/PpLF4rt4ah>
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Files: `soldier_parts.glb` (derived: the kit Soldier's rig, clips and meshes, split into parts and recoloured, plus
  original hair, hats, eyewear, scarf, bow tie, armband and letter patch built from primitives, and the female
  Soldier's variants of the head, torso and legs: the kit meshes reshaped, with original lashes, mouth, freckles and
  cheeks),
  `soldier_parts_atlas.png` and `parts.json` (original), all written by `tools/blender/build_soldier_parts.py`
- Credit: on the Credits screen (`game/ui/credits.tres`)

No attribution is required; the credit is given anyway. The original parts and the atlas are released under the
game's licence.
