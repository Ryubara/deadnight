# Boss zombie (the Projectionist)

- Base model: "Zombie" by Quaternius
- Source: <https://poly.pizza/m/VlXjG0N8Eg>
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Files: `zombie_boss.glb`, derived by `tools/blender/build_boss.py`: the kit zombie's mesh pushed into a bigger build,
  recoloured on its atlas, with a lamp and a film reel added (original), its 16 clips kept and five new clips authored
  from them; `clips.json` and `boss.json` are written by the same script. The two PNGs (colour atlas and emission
  map) are the textures inside the .glb, which Godot's import extracts beside it.

No attribution is required; the credit is given anyway (Credits screen).
