# Deadnight

One night at a 1958 drive-in theater. The last reel of the season shows something it shouldn't, and the audience does
not stay dead. Up to eight players (people; bots come on top) hold out round after round, buying their way deeper into
the lot while keeping the generator fed: the dead are faster in the dark, and the dark is where they come from.
