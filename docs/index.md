# Deadnight

One night at a 1958 drive-in theater. The last reel of the season shows something it shouldn't, and the audience does
not stay dead. Up to four players hold out round after round, buying their way deeper into the lot while keeping the
generator fed: light slows the dead, and the dark is where they come from.
