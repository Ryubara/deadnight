# Research

What we learned while building Deadnight, written by whoever learned it, while working. One page per subject, with
its sources linked; findings that apply to every game move to the GDStudio docs hub.

- [UI foundation](ui-foundation.md): the `gds_ui` addon: tokens, motion values, drawn components, shader transitions.
- [Greybox and captures](greybox-and-captures.md): lights on a big floor, CSG collision timing, top-down captures.
- [Settings](settings.md): the settings service, the slider, toggle and option selector, and what bit while building them.
- [Type specimens](type-specimens.md): rendering fonts we do not commit; variable fonts, tracking, layered neon.
- [Rules engine](rules-engine.md): the round, points and fuel rules as a server-first `RefCounted`: actions, events, snapshots.
- [Main menu and the drive-in skin](main-menu.md): the neon sign, the night sky, film grain, the menu screens.
